# AIRBNB SuperHost Classifier Predictor

## Problem Statement:
When traveling or staying in different boroughs in New York city many people often use Airbnb to find a place to stay thats cozy, well kept and the main reason people use Airbnb is it has more space and often cheaper than a hotel. When booking an Airbnb however most of the host that get recommended in your area are labeled as superhost which are top rated experincd host with special badges. However, some host get the superhost badge but the quality of their listing is subpar, and consumers experience various issues with the listing.

However, host that have good quality and care for their customers aren't labeled as a superhost therefore they wont be given priority when people are looking for Airbnb listings through Airbnb. The goal of this project is to not only build a classifier that predicts the likelihood of a host becoming a Superhost but also provide insights on features most important to becoming a Superhost.
